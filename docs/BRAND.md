# FoodLoop brand guide

A small, honest guide so the project looks and sounds the same on GitHub, LinkedIn, slides and in person. It describes the identity of a prototype, not a company.

## 1. Brand in one paragraph

FoodLoop is a careful helper that makes sure good food gets to people before it spoils, and that someone is always accountable for the decision. It is warm about the goal and strict about safety.

## 2. Archetype

**Primary: the Caregiver.** The brand exists to look after others (neighbours, food banks, farmers). Its instinct is protection and service, not disruption.
**Secondary: the Sage.** It earns trust with evidence, sources and plain admission of what it does not know.

What this means in practice:

| Caregiver | Sage |
|---|---|
| Starts from people who need food, not from the technology | Cites sources, shows the method |
| Calm and kind, never alarmist | Names limits before anyone asks |
| "Hold" is a respected outcome | "We don't know yet" is an acceptable answer |

**What it is not:** the Hero ("we will end food waste") or the Magician ("AI that sees spoilage"). Both over-promise.

## 3. Positioning

- **For** farms, grocers and food-rescue teams who see edible food heading to waste
- **FoodLoop is** a review-first workflow that proposes where food should go and records who confirmed it
- **Unlike** tools that let software decide, it keeps a person in charge and leaves a record anyone can check
- **Proof so far:** a working prototype and a first-place result at the UFV Community Ideation Jam 2026 (Future Ready Communities). No field results yet.

## 4. Name and meaning

**FoodLoop** has two ideas in it: the loop food travels (grown, checked, shared, used) and the loop of accountability (propose, review, confirm, record). Write it as one word with a capital L in the middle.

## 5. Voice

| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| Plain, short sentences | Jargon and hype |
| Specific (numbers with sources) | Vague ("massive impact") |
| Honest about limits | Defensive |
| Warm toward people | Cute about food waste or hunger |

Words to use: *propose, review, confirm, hold, rescue, redirect, record, prototype, evidence.*
Words to avoid: *revolutionary, disrupt, guarantee, AI-powered detection, blockchain, solves hunger, eliminates waste, safe, accurate* (unless a measured figure follows).

### Taglines (pick one per context)

- **Good food, checked by people, moved in time.** (primary)
- Spot it early. Check it twice. Share it fast.
- A loop that ends with someone eating, not something wasted.

### Boilerplate (one sentence)

> FoodLoop is an open-source prototype that proposes a safe route for food about to spoil, requires a human decision, and keeps a tamper-evident record of each step.

## 6. Messaging pillars

1. **People decide.** The model proposes, a person confirms, always.
2. **Safety first.** Uncertain or risky food is held, not guessed.
3. **Show your work.** Every step is logged and the log can be checked.
4. **Honest about maturity.** A prototype trained on public photos, not a validated safety tool.

## 7. Visual identity

These match the colours already used in the app (`foodloop/templates/base.html`).

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary dark | Forest ink | `#14201a` |
| Header and deep surfaces | Pine | `#12261e` |
| Primary action | Leaf | `#2f7d52` |
| Highlight and caution | Amber | `#e8a33d` |
| Background | Paper | `#f4f1e8` |
| Lines and borders | Stone | `#cfcabb` |
| Error | Alert red | `#b00000` |

Usage: Paper background, Forest ink text, Leaf for primary buttons, Amber only for notices and the prototype banner. Keep text contrast high. Check any new pair before using it.

**Typography:** system sans-serif stack (as in the app), so nothing needs to be downloaded. Headings semibold, body regular.

**Logo idea (not drawn yet):** a simple closed loop shaped like an open circle with one gap, and a leaf at the gap. The gap stands for the human check. Keep it one colour (Leaf on Paper, or Paper on Pine). Do not use clip-art fruit or a photo of real people.

**Imagery:** your own screenshots, your own diagrams, photos you took or have the right to use. Show the app and the ledger, not stock photos of overflowing bins.

## 8. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Put "Prototype" near any screenshot or demo | Present screenshots as a live product |
| Credit data sources next to each number | Use a statistic without a source and year |
| Say "first place at the UFV Community Ideation Jam 2026" | Imply funding, customers or an award for the software |
| Show the hold route and the review step | Show only the happy path |
| Keep the credit labelled "demo, no monetary value" | Call it a carbon credit, token or currency |

## 9. Where to use it

| Place | What to include |
|---|---|
| GitHub README | One-line description, loop diagram, status banner, limitations link |
| LinkedIn post | Story (idea, win, build), one screenshot, honest status line, link to repo |
| Slides | Pine background, one idea per slide, source under every number |
| Conversations | The 10-second version from `PITCH.md` |
